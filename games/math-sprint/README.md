# Math Sprint 60 (trivia / math sprint)

**File:** [`math-sprint.html`](math-sprint.html). Open it in any phone browser. There's nothing to install.

## How to play
- You have 60 seconds. Tap the right answer out of 4 big buttons.
- Each right answer is worth 10 points × your multiplier. Every 5 in a row adds ×1.
- A wrong answer costs **3 seconds** and resets your combo.
- Questions get harder as you go: + and − first, then ×, then ÷, then two-digit sums.
- **Today's question sequence is the same for everyone**, so scores are directly comparable.

## What makes it addictive
- **Fixed 60 seconds:** short, with a clear end, and easy to fit into a spare minute.
- **Same questions all day:** your second try is faster because you've seen them. Beating "today's best" feels attainable.
- **The combo multiplier rewards care over speed.** One slip costs a lot, which keeps you tense.
- **Built-in brag stats:** score, accuracy and best run.

## Come-back hook
A daily play streak, today's best and the all-time best are all saved in `localStorage`. The questions change every day.
