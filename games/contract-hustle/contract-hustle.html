How it works

• Each round: make one move (optional), then bid on a contract or take small private jobs.

• The city scores every bid on price, reputation and connections, plus how the mayor feels about you. Two rival firms bid too.

• Winning: you pay the build cost now and get paid your bid when it's finished. Lowball bids win more but leave a thin margin and a risk of cutting corners (cracks, bad press).

• Public Opinion of your firm moves with what you build and how you play. Under 35 the mayor only lets you see one contract; under 20 you're frozen out.

• Score = company value after 10 rounds: cash + half of any unpaid work + reputation × 2 + connections.
